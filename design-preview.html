FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. They open your link in whatever browser they already have, and order through WhatsApp — which they already use.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. Orders happen directly between you and your customer on WhatsApp. We charge a plan fee and nothing else.

What does the AI do to my product photos?

It corrects lighting, colour and framing, and places the product on a clean backdrop. It never redraws the product, and your original is always kept so you can revert.

Can I change my theme later?

Yes, any time, in one tap. Your products and link stay exactly the same.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes, on the Pro plan. On other plans your shop lives at sura.shop/your-shop-name.
